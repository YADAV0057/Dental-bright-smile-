Questions people ask

Will the treatment hurt?

No. We use gentle numbing gel and modern tools, so most patients feel only light pressure.

Do you work on weekends?

Yes. We are open Saturday 9 am to 5 pm. Sunday is for emergencies only.

Can I pay in instalments?

For treatments above ₹10,000 we offer 3 and 6 month no-cost EMI.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Our child-friendly room and gentle approach help kids feel at ease from the first visit.
